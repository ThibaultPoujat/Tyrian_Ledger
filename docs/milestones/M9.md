# M9 - Beginner Fast-Flip MVP

## Goal

Deliver a local, read-only Guild Wars 2 Trading Post assistant for a player who
does not know trading terminology, does not know what items to choose, and
wants a concrete manual action to take in game.

The core journey is:

1. The player opens the website and completes a short setup: available capital
   and comfort with risk.
2. The player presses Scan the market.
3. The application reads current public market data, ranks eligible
   opportunities, and returns at most five independent recommendations.
4. The player chooses one recommendation and follows its numbered in-game
   instructions manually.

The application never places orders, performs gameplay, or uses an account API
key. A target time is guidance, never a promise that an order will fill.

## Product decisions

### MVP scope

- The only supported horizon is fast flips: immediate action or a buy order
  intended to fill soon. Longer investment horizons are deliberately absent.
- Recommendations cover the whole public Trading Post market, not a watchlist.
- A player initiates every scan. There is no scheduled collection, automatic
  refresh, or historical market-data retention.
- A scan returns either a complete ranked set or no recommendations. The UI
  clearly explains cancellation, failure, and retry.
- Results are individual candidates rather than a portfolio. The player acts
  on one at a time.
- The main navigation contains Recommendations and Settings only. Historical,
  account, crafting, and personal-performance experiences are out of scope.

### Beginner preferences

Settings ask for:

- Initial capital in gold, silver, and copper.
- One risk level: Cautious, Balanced, or Adventurous.

The risk level determines all profitability and spend constraints:

| Risk level | Maximum capital per recommendation | Minimum modeled ROI | Minimum modeled profit |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Cautious | 10% | 5% | 10 silver |
| Balanced | 25% | 8% | 25 silver |
| Adventurous | 50% | 12% | 50 silver |

The selected quantity is the largest whole quantity that satisfies the risk
cap and one normal in-game stack limit. The player does not configure fees,
profit thresholds, liquidity metrics, a portfolio split, or a desired profit
amount in the MVP.

For M9, a normal item stack is a fixed product cap of 250. It is not inferred
from public item metadata: `/v2/items` supplies the item ID and display name,
but does not provide a per-item `max_stack` field.

### Recommendation routes

Every qualifying item can appear in either or both of these groups:

- Can act now: buy at one copper above the current best buy order, then list
  at one copper below the current cheapest sell listing.
- Place an order and wait: place a buy order at one copper above the current
  best buy order, then list at one copper below the current cheapest sell
  listing after it fills.

The difference is the market-depth evidence and practical action state, not a
claim that either order will fill. Each card states its exact quantity, buy
price, sale price, total cost, estimated fees, estimated profit, modeled ROI,
and the date/time of the completed scan.

The financial model uses integer copper. The built-in Guild Wars 2 fee policy
is 5% listing fee plus 10% exchange fee; exact fee rounding and any minimum
fee are tracked in VERIFY-013 and must be confirmed before the model is treated
as authoritative.

### Scan and market-data policy

The application follows a bounded, player-triggered public-data workflow:

1. Fetch the whole public item-ID index and current aggregate commerce prices.
2. Apply a cheap deterministic screen to find viable candidate items.
3. Fetch current detailed listings and item metadata only for a bounded
   finalist set, batching through the typed gateway.
4. Recompute all recommendation metrics from the same scan inputs.
5. Publish at most five ranked results only after all required finalist reads
   succeed.

The gateway remains the sole owner of ArenaNet URLs, request scheduling,
batching, retry/rate-limit behavior, and typed external DTOs. The product does
not persist market snapshots, order-book history, recommendations, or
partially completed scan results.

### User experience

The first visit shows short guided setup with plain-language definitions:
capital is the amount the player can spend, risk controls how much can be
committed to one suggestion, and an order is created manually in the in-game
Trading Post. Guidance is concise rather than a trading course.

Recommendations are grouped by action state, show no more than five cards, and
include explicit manual steps such as:

1. Open the Trading Post in Guild Wars 2 and search for the named item.
2. Create the shown buy order for the shown quantity and unit price.
3. For the immediate route, buy/list exactly as instructed; for the wait
   route, return after the buy order fills.
4. Create the shown sell listing. The displayed profit is an estimate, not a
   guarantee.

No copy button, account linking, transaction importing, order tracking, or
post-trade profit reconciliation is included.

## Completion rule

M9 is complete when a new player can set capital and risk, trigger a complete
public-market scan, receive at most five transparent fast-flip suggestions,
and manually follow the listed in-game steps without needing trading knowledge.
All behavior is deterministic, accessible, read-only, adequately tested, and
does not retain historical market or personal account data.

## Non-goals

- Guaranteed fill times, profit, or price stability.
- Long-horizon investment advice or historical-market analytics.
- Account API keys, account inventory, crafting, wallet, transactions, or
  personal trade history.
- Automated Trading Post actions or gameplay automation.
- User-configured financial formulas or an in-application LLM.
