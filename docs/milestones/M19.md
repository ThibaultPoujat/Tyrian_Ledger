# M19 - Core Recommendation Product

## Goal

Combine current market evidence, owned history, personal state, and bankroll
risk into the primary deterministic `What Should I Do?` workflow.

## Ticket sequence

| Ticket | Scope | Depends on |
|---|---|---|
| [TKT-M19-01](M19/tickets/TKT-M19-01.md) | Historical spread, volatility, persistence, liquidity analytics with sufficiency/coverage. | M18-02 |
| [TKT-M19-02](M19/tickets/TKT-M19-02.md) | Explainable multi-factor opportunity score and anomaly flags. | M17-02, M19-01 |
| [TKT-M19-03](M19/tickets/TKT-M19-03.md) | Bankroll-aware position sizing, cash reserve, liquidity and concentration limits. | M15-03, M17-02, M19-02 |
| [TKT-M19-04](M19/tickets/TKT-M19-04.md) | Primary explicit-action recommendation engine/API/UI. | M16-01, M17-03, M19-01, M19-02, M19-03 |

## Completion rule

The user can review deterministic BUY/WAIT/order-management/sell/hold actions in
about one or two minutes, see their evidence and constraints, and no action path
can execute a trade automatically.
