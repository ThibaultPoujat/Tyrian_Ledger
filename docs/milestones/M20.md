# M20 - Personal Learning and Investments

## Goal

Use observed personal outcomes to improve capital-turnover understanding and
support medium/long-term positions without corrupting short-term accounting.

## Ticket sequence

| Ticket | Scope | Depends on |
|---|---|---|
| [TKT-M20-01](M20/tickets/TKT-M20-01.md) | Approximate fill/holding time and capital-turnover intelligence with timestamp limitations. | M14-02, M15-03, M18-02 |

| [TKT-M20-03](M20/tickets/TKT-M20-03.md) | Seasonal/speculative position tracking, targets, staged exits, opportunity-cost context, and collector-priority integration. | M15-03, M19-01, M19-03 |

## Completion rule

Personal evidence influences recommendations only with disclosed sufficient
samples, and investment positions have explicit thesis/targets/current value
without being mistaken for guaranteed appreciation or short-term flip results.
