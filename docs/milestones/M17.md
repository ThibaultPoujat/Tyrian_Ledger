# M17 - Live Market Intelligence

## Goal

Turn the proven public-market/analytics foundation into a current fee-aware,
liquidity-aware scanner that produces usable candidates and watchlists.

## Ticket sequence

| Ticket | Scope | Depends on |
|---|---|---|
| [TKT-M17-01](M17/tickets/TKT-M17-01.md) | Live fee-aware scanner with exact net profit/ROI/max bid and basic filters. | M13-01, M15-01 |
| [TKT-M17-02](M17/tickets/TKT-M17-02.md) | Detailed order-book depth, price impact, shallow-market flags, liquidity-aware sizing evidence. | M17-01 |
| [TKT-M17-03](M17/tickets/TKT-M17-03.md) | Scanner React UI, filters, detail, and local watchlist workflow. | M17-02 |

## Completion rule

The user can discover current candidates with exact economics and visible
liquidity risk; raw ROI alone cannot make shallow junk look best.
