# TKT-M19-03 - Add Bankroll-Aware Position Sizing, Cash Reserve, and Concentration Risk

GitHub issue: #87

## Milestone

M19 - Core Recommendation Product

## Goal

Turn market attractiveness into explainable capital allocations that respect
available cash, existing exposure, market liquidity, and diversification.

## Functional outcome

The app can suggest a bounded quantity/capital for an opportunity and explain
which cap constrained it, while preserving cash reserve and preventing one
market/strategy from consuming the portfolio.

## Dependencies

TKT-M15-03, TKT-M17-02, TKT-M19-02.

## Recommended Codex configuration

Risk class: **R3 (financial/risk)**.

- Implementation: Sol High preferred.
- Review: fresh flagship XHigh.

## Initial configurable policy concepts

Reference starting values, not hidden constants:

- cash reserve ~15%;
- high-liquidity single-market cap up to ~5% bankroll;
- medium-liquidity ~2.5-3%;
- low-liquidity/speculative ~1-2%;
- additional cap from order-book/depth participation;
- strategy/category concentration warnings.

## Requirements

- Compute deployable capital after reserve/current orders/positions.
- Existing exposure counts toward caps.
- Suggested size is min of independent cash/item/liquidity/concentration caps.
- Unknown/negative bankroll state fails safely.
- Expose binding constraints/reasons.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Sizing scales with bankroll in representative vectors.
- [ ] Single attractive market cannot consume portfolio.
- [ ] Equivalent illiquid market sizes smaller than liquid one.
- [ ] Cash reserve cannot be allocated through composition.
- [ ] Existing orders/positions reduce remaining capacity.
- [ ] Multiple simultaneous candidates/concentration tests exist.

## Validation

- Run independently derived bankroll scaling, cash-reserve, existing exposure,
  liquidity, item, strategy/category concentration, simultaneous-candidate,
  negative, and unknown-state vectors.
- Verify suggested size is the minimum of all applicable caps and explanations
  identify every binding constraint.
- Run fee, order-book, accounting, and score prerequisite suites.

## Non-goals

Automated capital deployment, final action-screen composition, or hidden policy
constants.
